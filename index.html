What we do

Four disciplines. One point of contact.

Brokerage

We source and manage the right hauler for every stream, at the right price. One relationship replaces a dozen vendor calls.

Compliance

Waste transfer notes, duty of care, and audit trails, kept current and ready for inspection without you chasing paperwork.

Contract & tender support

We prepare, benchmark, and negotiate contracts on your behalf, so terms favour your business, not the incumbent hauler.

Reporting

Clear, periodic reporting on volumes, diversion, and cost. What leaves your site, where it goes, and what it achieves.

The commitments

Small, considered. Kept every time.

  • Paperless by design

    Every note, ticket, and report lives online. Nothing to file, nothing to lose.

  • A tree planted per contract

    Every new client contract funds a tree, planted on their behalf.

  • Local-first placement

    We route waste to the nearest suitable facility first. Fewer miles, better outcomes.

  • Every tonne tracked

    What's diverted from landfill is measured and recorded, not estimated.

How it works
