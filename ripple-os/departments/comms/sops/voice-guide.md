# SOP — Voice guide

**Purpose.** Everything Ripple sends outward sounds like one person wrote it:
Tadhg. Derived from Ripple's own site copy — *"Waste, handled properly."*

`[ASSUMPTION]` This guide is reverse-engineered from the website, not dictated by
Tadhg. Correct anything that doesn't sound like you; this file is the source of
truth once corrected.

## The voice, in five lines

1. **Plain.** Short sentences. One idea each.
2. **Understated.** Claims are small and kept, not large and hedged.
3. **Concrete.** Numbers, dates, codes. Not "significant savings" — the figure.
4. **Unhurried.** No urgency tricks, no scarcity, no pressure.
5. **Direct.** Say the thing, including when the thing is bad.

## Site copy this is drawn from

> "Waste, handled properly."
> "Four disciplines. One point of contact."
> "Small, considered. Kept every time."
> "The audit is the front door."
> "No site visit, no sales call. Send three things, and receive a clear picture
> of where you stand."

Short declaratives. Full stops where a lesser writer uses a comma. No adjective
doing work a number could do.

## Never write

| Banned | Why |
| --- | --- |
| ! | Not the voice. Ever. |
| "I hope this email finds you well" | Nobody means it |
| "Just checking in", "circling back", "bumping this" | Says nothing |
| "market-leading", "bespoke", "seamless", "hassle-free", "solutions" | Adjectives instead of facts |
| "As per my last email" | Passive aggression |
| "Feel free to reach out" | Filler |
| "we collect / we haul / our trucks" | **False.** Ripple arranges. It is a broker. |
| Emoji | No |
| "Kind regards," + three-line signature | Too much |

## Structure of a reply

1. Answer the question, first sentence.
2. Whatever context is needed. Two or three sentences at most.
3. One clear next step, with a date.
4. Sign off: `Tadhg` — or `Thanks, Tadhg`. Nothing more.

## Worked example

**Wrong**

> Hi James, I hope this email finds you well! Thanks so much for reaching out to
> Ripple Recycling — we'd be absolutely delighted to help with your waste
> requirements. We offer bespoke, market-leading solutions and our trucks can
> collect from your site at a time that suits you! Just let me know and I'll get
> that sorted right away!!

**Right**

> James,
>
> Yes, we can arrange that. Three things I need first: the largest vehicle that
> can get onto the yard, whether the bins can be out before 09:00, and your SIC
> code for the transfer notes.
>
> Send those over and I'll come back with a price on Thursday.
>
> Tadhg

## Bad news

Say it first, plainly, then what happens next. No cushioning.

> The collection on Tuesday didn't happen — the carrier couldn't get access
> before 10:00. I've moved it to Thursday morning and there's no charge for the
> failed visit.

Not: *"Unfortunately, it appears there may have been an issue..."*

## Compliance language

Ripple **arranges** movements. It does not collect, carry, store or treat waste.
Wording that suggests otherwise is a legal misstatement, not a style slip.

Correct: *"We'll arrange collection with a licensed carrier."*
Wrong: *"We'll collect on Thursday."*
